Who can partner with Fusion Foods?

We work with established fast-moving consumer goods distributors, regional retail stores, institutional bulk buyers, and raw-material agricultural vendors.

What is your manufacturing capacity?

Our Madanapalle production plant operates at a stated capacity of 200 kilograms per day. Order allocations, delivery pacing, and custom batch scheduling are confirmed directly.

Do you offer customized packaging formats?

We are open to discussing tailored wholesale package sizes and commercial branding options. Connect with our team to evaluate volume feasibility.

Fusion Foods adheres strictly to FSSAI food-safety guidelines, conducting routine inspections across raw potato sorting, oil quality checks, and clean nitrogen packing.
