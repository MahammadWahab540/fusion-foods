From potato to pack.

Consistent crunch does not happen by accident. It is the outcome of disciplined handling at every production milestone.

01

Clean & Prepare

Fresh raw potatoes are carefully inspected, destoned, washed, and peeled in controlled hygienic batches.

02

Slice & Fry

Uniform precision rotary slicing ensures identical thickness before flash-frying at monitored temperatures.

03

Inspect & Season

Trained operators continuously audit moisture, golden color, and even seasoning distribution.

04

Pack with Care

Aroma-tight, moisture-barrier packaging preserves long-lasting crunch from our unit to your retail shelves.
